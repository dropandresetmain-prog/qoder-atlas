# Employer travel policy (Northgate Consulting Group)

Scope: all employee travel booked through the appointed TMC.

1. Spend ceiling: flight spend per domestic trip must not exceed USD 400.
2. Approvals: every flight change or cancellation requires travel-manager
   (organisation approver) sign-off before ticketing, regardless of amount.
3. Cabin: economy only for domestic trips under four hours.
4. Duty of care: the TMC must keep the employer travel desk informed of any
   traveller stranded overnight.
