# Invited-speaker travel policy (DevSummit 2026)

Scope: travel arranged for invited speakers.

1. Spend ceiling: recovery/rebooking spend per speaker must not exceed
   USD 1,500 in total.
2. Approval: fare differences above USD 300 require organiser travel-desk
   approval before ticketing. Below the threshold the traveller's own
   confirmation is sufficient.
3. Rebooking costs: the organiser covers reasonable rebooking fees for
   invited speakers when the disruption is carrier-caused.
4. Cabin: economy unless the programme committee decides otherwise.
