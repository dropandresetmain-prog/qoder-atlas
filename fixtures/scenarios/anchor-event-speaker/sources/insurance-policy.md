# Speaker travel protection policy (extract)

Insured: invited speakers travelling under the DevSummit programme.

Coverage:
- Trip delay: reasonable additional transport/accommodation costs after a
  carrier-caused delay or cancellation.
- Missed connection: onward transport to reach the insured obligation.
- Travel supplier insolvency: unused prepaid trip costs.

Limits:
- Excess: USD 50 per claim.
- Maximum payout: USD 1,200 per traveller per trip.

Claims are filed after the trip; this policy informs recovery options but
claims automation is out of scope.
