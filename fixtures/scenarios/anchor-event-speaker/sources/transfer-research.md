# Transfer research note (aggregated, sourced)

Route: Narita International Airport -> Bayside Conference Hotel district.

- Typical taxi/ridehail duration: 50–60 minutes off-peak, up to 80 minutes
  during evening congestion (sourced travel-time aggregation, medium quality).
- Trains exist but require one change plus a short walk with luggage.
- On-demand options are available around the clock; no advance booking needed.

This is an operational estimate with uncertainty, not a guaranteed time.
