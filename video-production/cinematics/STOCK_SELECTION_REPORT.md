# NORTHSTAR cinematic stock selection report

Date: 2026-08-28
Scope: real, free stock footage only. No AI-generated footage, paid libraries, final-film edits, or NORTHSTAR system graphics were created in this lane.

## Licence and selection standard

All retained footage is hosted by Pexels and marked free to use under the [Pexels licence](https://www.pexels.com/license/). Source pages and the downloaded files should remain associated in editorial records. The files are intentionally ignored by Git; only this selection record and directory layout are versioned.

Selection bar: real observational footage; horizontal 16:9; at least 1920 x 1080; a clear relationship to the shot slot; no posed-to-camera business stock, handshakes, fake hacker imagery, or tourism-ad framing. For 1–3 second uses, the editor should take a short, motivated excerpt rather than use a full source clip.
